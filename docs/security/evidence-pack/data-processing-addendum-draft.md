# OASIS data processing addendum — contract draft

**Not for signature until completed and reviewed by qualified German and Indian
data-protection counsel.** This draft records the intended controller/processor
relationship and is not a substitute for advice on a school's specific duties.

OASIS is designed to support schools' compliance with applicable data-protection
and student-privacy requirements, including GDPR, India's DPDP framework, COPPA
and FERPA. Applicability depends on the school, jurisdiction and deployment. OASIS
is an adult-only educator service, does not sell learner data or use it for
advertising, and does not make solely automated high-impact decisions. CIPA
obligations remain with eligible schools and libraries. OASIS is not a
healthcare-record system and must not be used to store protected health information
or medical records. Formal compliance remains subject to completed contracts,
school approval, legal review and the controls identified in the OASIS readiness
plan.

## 1. Definitions and law

Terms including controller, processor, personal data, processing, data subject,
personal data breach and supervisory authority have the meanings in the EU General
Data Protection Regulation 2016/679 (**GDPR**) where it applies. References to
**Data Protection Law** include the GDPR, Germany's Federal Data Protection Act and
applicable state/education law, India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023
and rules as they enter into force, and any other mandatory law applicable to the
processing.

**School Data** means personal data processed by OASIS on the Controller's behalf.
It excludes personal data for which OASIS independently determines the purposes,
such as its business-account administration, security, billing and legal records,
which OASIS processes under its privacy notice.

## 2. Roles and instructions

The Controller determines the purposes and essential means of processing School
Data. OASIS processes School Data only on the Controller's documented instructions,
including this Addendum and authorised use of the service, unless law requires
otherwise. If legally permitted, OASIS will notify the Controller before processing
required by law.

OASIS will promptly inform the Controller if an instruction appears to infringe
Data Protection Law and may suspend that instruction while the parties resolve it.
The Controller remains responsible for lawful collection, notices, lawful basis,
children/guardian requirements, user authorisation and the accuracy of its data.

## 4. Confidentiality and personnel

OASIS will limit School Data access to people who need it to operate, secure or
support the service. Authorised personnel will be bound by confidentiality, receive
role-appropriate privacy/security training and use individual accounts. Access will
be reviewed and removed promptly when no longer needed.

## 5. Security

Taking into account the state of the art, cost, processing context and risks—
especially risks to children—OASIS will maintain appropriate technical and
organisational measures, including:

- encryption in transit and provider-supported encryption at rest;
- school/class tenant isolation, least privilege and role-based access;
- individual accounts, privileged MFA, session controls and access reviews;
- learner pseudonymisation/minimisation and private file storage;
- secure development, dependency scanning, secret handling and change review;
- security audit records without learner narrative content;
- incident response, monitoring and vulnerability remediation; and
- monitored backups, restoration tests and continuity arrangements once the
  release-gate controls documented in the evidence pack are completed.

Current controls and open release gates are described in the [evidence pack
index](README.md). A draft or open item is not a contractual claim that the control
is operational.

## 6. Subprocessors

The Controller gives general written authorisation for OASIS to use the
subprocessors in the current [Subprocessor register](subprocessors.md). OASIS will:

1. contractually require each subprocessor to protect School Data to a standard
   materially consistent with this Addendum;
2. remain responsible for its subprocessor obligations as required by law;
3. maintain an up-to-date list and give `[30]` days' notice of a new or replacement
   subprocessor, except where urgent security/continuity needs make prior notice
   impracticable; and
4. provide a reasonable opportunity to object on substantiated privacy grounds.

If the parties cannot resolve an objection using a reasonable alternative, the
Controller may stop the affected feature or terminate it under the Agreement.

## 7. International transfers

OASIS will not transfer School Data internationally without a lawful mechanism.
Where EEA personal data is transferred to a country without an adequacy decision,
the parties incorporate the European Commission's 2021 Standard Contractual
Clauses, using Module Two for controller-to-processor and Module Three where the
Controller itself acts as a processor. The Controller is data exporter; OASIS is
data importer; Irish law and Irish courts are proposed for the SCC choices, subject
to counsel confirmation and the main Agreement.

The parties will document transfer locations, assess relevant laws and implement
supplementary safeguards where required. Transfers from India will comply with any
notified restriction applicable at the time. Provider contractual mechanisms must
be verified for the subscribed plan before production use.

## 8. Data-subject requests

Taking account of the processing, OASIS will provide reasonable technical and
organisational assistance for access, correction, erasure, restriction, objection,
portability and complaint requests. OASIS will not independently respond about
School Data unless authorised or legally required and will route unverified/direct
requests to the Controller. The [Deletion and export
procedure](deletion-and-export-procedure.md) applies.

## 9. DPIAs and regulatory cooperation

OASIS will provide information reasonably available to it to help the Controller
meet security, breach, DPIA and prior-consultation duties. The Controller owns and
approves its DPIA; the OASIS template is supporting evidence, not the Controller's
final decision.

## 10. Personal data breaches

OASIS will notify the Controller without undue delay after confirming a personal
data breach affecting School Data. Notice will include, as available, its nature,
affected categories/approximate numbers, likely consequences, measures taken or
proposed, and an incident contact. Information may be delivered in phases.

OASIS will preserve evidence, contain the incident, cooperate with reasonable
Controller instructions and not notify learners/families or authorities on the
Controller's behalf without authorisation unless legally required. The internal
[Incident response plan](incident-response.md) applies. Contract-specific notice
target: `[PROPOSED: initial notice within 24 hours of confirmation]`.

## 11. Audit and information

OASIS will make information reasonably necessary to demonstrate compliance with
this Addendum available to the Controller, normally through the evidence pack,
provider assurance reports and written responses. No more than once annually,
unless required after an incident or by a regulator, the Controller may request a
reasonable audit by an independent qualified auditor subject to confidentiality,
security, tenant protection, advance notice and cost terms in the Agreement.

Audits must not expose another customer's data, security secrets or provider
information OASIS is not permitted to disclose.

## 12. Return, deletion and termination

On expiry or the Controller's verified instruction, OASIS will return/export School
Data in the available format and delete remaining School Data, unless law requires
retention. Provider backups will expire under their documented cycle; retained
copies remain protected and unavailable for ordinary use.

The final Agreement must specify the export window, deletion completion target,
backup expiry and any fees. Current product limitations are stated in the [Deletion
and export procedure](deletion-and-export-procedure.md) and must be resolved or
expressly accepted before signature.

## 13. Controller obligations for children

The Controller will determine whether and how parental/guardian authorisation,
education-law authority or another lawful basis applies; provide age-appropriate
information where required; restrict user access; and prevent use of OASIS for
diagnosis, safeguarding case management or solely automated significant decisions.

## 14. Liability, governing law and precedence

Liability and governing law follow the Agreement except where mandatory Data
Protection Law or incorporated transfer clauses require otherwise. Counsel must
complete this clause and all bracketed fields before signature.
